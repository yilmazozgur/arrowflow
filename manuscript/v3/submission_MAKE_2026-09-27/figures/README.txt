ArrowFlow figure files for MAKE

Figures 1-8 belong to the main manuscript. Figures S1-S5 belong to the supplement.
Each figure is supplied as a complete 600-dpi RGB PNG with its panels combined,
and as a vector PDF. Captions are in the manuscripts. These files are rendered
from the same figure sources included in ArrowFlow_sources_MAKE.zip.
figure_manifest.json maps printed numbers to source files and PNG checksums.
